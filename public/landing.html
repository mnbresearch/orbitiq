Capabilities

Six disciplines, one operating picture.

Each module runs against the same live catalogue and the same physics, so what you see in one view reconciles with every other. No exports, no stitching, no waiting for a daily report.

◎

Conjunction screening

Continuous all-on-all screening across the tracked catalogue, with probability of collision computed per encounter and every close approach ranked by consequence rather than raw miss distance.

▲

Fleet risk

Your spacecraft, scored and ordered. Risk is aggregated per asset and per constellation plane, so an operations lead can see where attention belongs in a single glance.

↓

Re-entry forecasting

Decaying objects tracked with lifetime estimates that respond to atmospheric density, giving you advance notice of what is coming down and roughly when.

☀

Space weather

Geomagnetic and solar-flux conditions folded directly into drag and decay modelling, plus operational context on when the environment is degrading your predictions.

⌁

Contact planning

Ground-station pass scheduling with elevation, duration and Doppler, so command windows and conjunction timelines can be reasoned about together.

◈

Manoeuvre & anomaly detection

Orbital element histories are differenced over time to surface manoeuvres and unexplained changes across the objects that matter to you.

The difference

Anyone can show you the sky today. We can show you what it did.

Public catalogues are a snapshot — they tell you where objects are right now and forget everything else. OrbitIQ writes every detection into a permanent intelligence archive at the moment it happens. That record cannot be reconstructed later from public sources, by us or by anyone else.

  • →Every conjunction, congestion shift, re-entry and environmental event is archived at detection time.
  • →Trends are measured against what we actually observed, not back-filled from today's catalogue.
  • →An independent pipeline refreshes the catalogue every six hours, so the picture never silently goes stale.
  • →The longer the platform runs, the less replaceable the history becomes.
“The value is not in knowing that two objects will pass close. It is in being able to prove, six months later, exactly what the sky looked like when you made the decision to manoeuvre.”
— Design principle, OrbitIQ intelligence archive

Capabilities

Six disciplines, one operating picture.

Each module runs against the same live catalogue and the same physics, so what you see in one view reconciles with every other. No exports, no stitching, no waiting for a daily report.

The difference

Anyone can show you the sky today. We can show you what it did.

Public catalogues are a snapshot — they tell you where objects are right now and forget everything else. OrbitIQ writes every detection into a permanent intelligence archive at the moment it happens. That record cannot be reconstructed later from public sources, by us or by anyone else.

  • 01Every conjunction, congestion shift, re-entry and environmental event is archived at detection time.
  • 02Trends are measured against what we actually observed, not back-filled from today's catalogue.
  • 03An independent pipeline refreshes the catalogue every six hours, so the picture never silently goes stale.
  • 04The longer the platform runs, the less replaceable the history becomes.
“The value is not in knowing that two objects will pass close. It is in being able to prove, six months later, exactly what the sky looked like when you made the decision to manoeuvre.”
— Design principle, OrbitIQ intelligence archive

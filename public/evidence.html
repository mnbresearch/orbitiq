Who can verify your record

A grant gives a named third party — an insurer, a regulator, a prime — read access to the evidence above through the verifier console. It is scoped, time-boxed and revocable, and there is no endpoint on that path that can alter what you recorded.

Issue a grant

Read-only access to your evidence for a named third party.
Required. A grant issued to nobody in particular cannot be audited later, which is the whole reason for having one.
The record window bounds what they can see; the validity period bounds how long they can see it. Both are capped, because an access right nobody remembers issuing is one nobody remembers revoking.
